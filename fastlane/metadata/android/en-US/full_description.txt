Clio is a clean, simple audiobook player for Android designed for listening to local audio files without clutter, tracking, or account requirements.

Features:
* Modern Material Design 3 interface with dynamic theming
* Audiobook queue with drag-and-drop reordering
* Fast library search and folder-based organization
* Sleep timer with custom fade-out duration
* Built-in 5-band equalizer with presets for spoken voice and vocal clarity
* Playback speed control
* Listening statistics and progress tracking
* Screen playback lock against accidental touches
* Completely open source, ad-free and privacy-friendly
