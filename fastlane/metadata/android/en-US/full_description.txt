Clio is a clean, customizable open-source audiobook player for Android.

Audio & Playback Control
* Built-in equalizer with spoken voice presets (Vocal Clarity, Treble Boost, Bass Cut, De-Esser)
* Independent skip intervals for rewind and fast-forward (e.g. 30s forward, 10s backward)
* Auto-rewind after pauses and skip silence support
* Volume boost for quiet recordings
* Variable playback speed

Smart Library & Queue
* Flexible audiobook queue with drag & drop reordering
* Folder-based organization (single book, author, and series structures)
* Bookmark feature with timestamps
* Automatic resume: open your last played book directly on startup
* Fast library search, multi-selection, and cover art picker

Intelligent Sleep Timer
* Manual or scheduled auto-timer (e.g. active during night hours)
* Smooth fade-out and stop at end of chapter
* Shake to extend

Detailed Listening Statistics
* Monthly and yearly overview of your listening habits
* Seamless import of existing history from "Smart AudioBook Player"

Design & Personalization
* Customizable player interface
* Customizable grid layout
* Touch lock to prevent accidental input in your pocket
* Versatile player backgrounds (blurred cover, dynamic gradients, AMOLED black, glassmorphism)
* Material You & Themes (Catppuccin Mocha, OLED black, hex color picker)
